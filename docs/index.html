Know where your money goes. Plan where it should go.

An AI-assisted personal finance tracker for India. Record spending, set budgets and goals, track loans and EMIs, import bank statements, and get plain-language answers about your own numbers.

Live demo coming soon View the code

The demo will use made-up data only. No real accounts or money.

What it does

Everything below is built into the app.

Transactions & categories

Expenses, income, transfers and refunds across daily, weekly, monthly and yearly views, with search and filters.

Budgets & goals

Daily to yearly budgets with threshold alerts, plus savings goals with contributions and progress.

Loans & EMIs

Reducing-balance EMI schedules, what is still owed, interest paid and left, and the payoff date.

Statement & receipt import

CSV, Excel, PDF and receipt images are previewed first, with duplicate detection, before anything is saved.

AI analysis & assistant

Monthly insights and a chat that answers from your recorded numbers, with a built-in fallback when no AI key is set.

Savings planner & reports

A monthly savings plan, a what-if simulator, and PDF/CSV exports.

Bank linking (Account Aggregator)

Consent-based, read-only linking through an RBI-regulated Account Aggregator. Built for the Setu sandbox; the demo shows seeded banks.

Built with

Money is stored as integer paise; every user's data is isolated by database row-level security.
